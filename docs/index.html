What's inside

Everything runs locally on your device—no cloud proxies, no ads, no speed caps.

32-Segment Engine

Splits large HTTP downloads into parallel byte-range connections with automatic resume, queues, and night scheduling.

4K Media Grabber

Download video and MP3 audio from YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Spotify, SoundCloud, and X with bundled yt-dlp & FFmpeg.

Song ID & Browser Sync

Identify background songs from any clip using Shazam fingerprinting, and capture browser downloads automatically.

Quick Setup & Usage

Select your platform to get started in under a minute.

1

Install & Launch

Run GRabbit-Setup-1.2.1.exe (or open the portable build). Both yt-dlp and FFmpeg are included automatically.

2

Grab Media or Direct Files

Use Downloads → + Add for direct HTTP files (ISO, ZIP, RAR) or open Grab Media to paste a YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, or Spotify link and pick your quality.

3

Quick Grab Shortcut

Press Ctrl + Alt + G anywhere in Windows to open the floating Quick Grab bar without switching windows.

1

Unzip the Extension Package

Download GRabbit-Chrome-Extension-v1.2.1.zip above and extract it to a folder on your PC.

2

Load Unpacked in Your Browser

Open chrome://extensions , turn on Developer mode (top-right), click Load unpacked, and select the extracted folder.

3

Grab Videos & Intercept Downloads

Keep GRabbit Desktop open—the extension connects automatically on port 31542. Click the floating Download with GRabbit button on videos or right-click any link.

1

Install the APK

Download GRabbit-v1.2.1.apk on your phone and open it to install (allow Install unknown apps if prompted).

2

Share from YouTube, Instagram, or TikTok

Tap Share → GRabbit inside any social media app to immediately inspect and download video or audio streams.

3

Play Offline or Sync with Desktop

Play completed downloads in the built-in media player, export to your Gallery, or forward downloads to your PC via Settings → Desktop RPC Bridge.
