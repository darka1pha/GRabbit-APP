Step-by-Step Documentation

How to Install & Use GRabbit

Select a component below for complete setup and usage instructions.

1

Install & Launch GRabbit

Download and run GRabbit-Setup-1.0.0.exe (or launch GRabbit-1.0.0-portable.exe). Both yt-dlp and FFmpeg are bundled inside—no extra tools required.

Default Save Path: Downloads\GRabbit
2

Multi-Connection Direct Downloads

Click + Add Download in the Downloads tab and paste any direct file URL (ISO, ZIP, RAR, EXE, MP4). GRabbit splits the file into up to 32 parallel byte-range segments.

Up to 32 Connections • Auto-Resume
3

Global Quick Grab Pill (Ctrl+Alt+G)

Copy any link anywhere in Windows—GRabbit's clipboard monitor notifies you automatically, or press Ctrl + Alt + G at any time to summon the floating Quick Grab Pill.

Shortcut: Ctrl + Alt + G
4

Queues, Scheduler & Speed Limits

Organize tasks into custom queues (e.g., Night Queue), configure start/stop schedules (e.g., 02:00 → 08:00), and set an after-queue action (Quit, Sleep, or Shutdown PC).

Settings → Downloads → Scheduler
1

Download & Extract the ZIP

Download GRabbit-Chrome-Extension-v1.0.0.zip from the Downloads section above and extract (unzip) it to a permanent folder on your PC (or use packages/extension/dist if building from source).

Extract to e.g. C:\GRabbit-Extension
2

Load Unpacked in Chrome / Edge / Brave

Open your browser's Extensions page, toggle Developer mode ON in the top-right corner, click Load unpacked (top-left), and select the extracted folder containing manifest.json.

chrome://extensions
3

Verify "Connected" Status

Make sure GRabbit Desktop is running on your PC. Click the GRabbit icon in your browser toolbar—the badge in the top-right will show 🟢 Connected automatically on port 31542.

Bridge: http://127.0.0.1:31542
4

Grab Videos & Auto-Intercept

• Hover any video on YouTube/Instagram/TikTok and click 🐰 Download with GRabbit.
• Right-click any link or page and choose Download with GRabbit.
• Browser file downloads are automatically captured and accelerated by GRabbit Desktop.

💡 Custom Token Pairing (Optional): If you regenerate your security token in GRabbit Desktop → Settings → Browser Extension, click the Copy button in Desktop Settings, open the Chrome Extension's ⚙️ Settings (Options) page, paste the token, and click Save Settings.
1

Install GRabbit-v1.0.0.apk

Download GRabbit-v1.0.0.apk on your Android phone and tap to install. If prompted by Android or Xiaomi MIUI, allow "Install unknown apps" for your browser or file manager.

Supports Android 8.0 – 15 & Xiaomi MIUI
2

Share Directly from Other Apps

While watching a video in the YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, or X app, tap Share → GRabbit. GRabbit Mobile opens automatically and resolves all available video/audio qualities.

Share Sheet → GRabbit
3

Play & Save to Gallery

Preview streams or play completed downloads with the built-in Video/Audio player, inspect byte segments, verify SHA-256 checksums, and tap Save to Library to export to your phone's Gallery.

Downloads Tab → Play / Save to Photos
4

Optional: Connect to Desktop over Wi-Fi

In Settings → Desktop RPC Bridge, enter your PC's local IP address (e.g. 192.168.1.x), port 31542, and token to send heavy downloads directly from your phone to your PC!

Settings → Test Desktop Connection
1

Grab 4K Video or MP3 Audio

Open the Grab Media tab, paste any YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, X/Twitter, SoundCloud, or Vimeo URL, and choose your preferred resolution (up to 4K/8K) or audio format (MP3 / M4A).

2

Playlists & Spotify Matching

Paste a full YouTube playlist or Spotify track/playlist URL. GRabbit lists every item so you can select individual tracks or batch-download the entire playlist with one click.

3

Shazam Song Recognition

Heard a background song in a Reel, TikTok, or local file? Open the Recognize tab, paste the video URL or pick a local file, and GRabbit fingerprints the audio to identify the title, artist, album art, and lyrics.

4

Private / Age-Restricted Videos

Need to grab members-only or age-restricted media? Go to Settings → Media and select your browser under Read Cookies from Browser (or attach a cookies.txt file).
